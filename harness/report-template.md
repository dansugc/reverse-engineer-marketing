# Report format (Markdown)

Write the report in exactly these sections, in this order. Use the company name in the title.

# How {Company} gets customers

**One-line verdict:** the engine in one sentence (e.g. "Paid Meta UGC at scale feeding a free-trial app funnel, with founder-led X for credibility").

## TL;DR
3–5 bullets. Each bullet = a play + the proof + why it works. Cite evidence.

## Channel scorecard
A table: Channel | Status | Strength (0–3) | What we saw | Evidence. Use the provided scorecard rows (skip rows that are unknown with strength 0 unless notable) and sharpen the "what we saw" text.

## The engine
How the channels connect into a loop. Name the entry offer, the main CTA, the landing pages, and how traffic is converted and retained.

## Paid
Per platform with evidence: volume, longevity of winners, formats (video/image/carousel/UGC), hooks and angles (quote the best 3–5), CTAs, landing pages, UTM naming, who is the target. Call out the longest-running ads explicitly: those are the proven winners.

## Organic social
Per platform: cadence, reach, formats, the hooks that win (quote top posts), whether organic winners are reused as ads.

## SEO & AI search
Site size, programmatic page families and their templates, content cadence, money pages (top Google results), comparison/alternative pages, AI-crawler stance and llms.txt.

## Founders & people
Who the founders are (name, role, confidence and where the name came from), their audience size, whether they post, what they post about, and how that feeds the company. Never present a low-confidence name as fact.

## Reddit
Is Reddit worked on purpose? Brand/founder posts and replies (quote them, name subreddits), owned subreddit and its activity, comparison and "what should I use" threads they appear in, organic recommendations, Reddit threads ranking on Google, Reddit pixel, sentiment.

## Community & launches
HN / Product Hunt launches, owned communities (Discord, Slack), ambassador programs.

## Funnel & monetization
Pricing model, entry offer, trial/free plan, sales-led vs self-serve, lifecycle/email stack, affiliate/referral program and terms, social proof used.

## Positioning over time
How the homepage pitch changed (from the Wayback history) and what the changes suggest.

## Steal this playbook
A numbered list of 5–10 concrete steps a founder could run in the next 30 days to copy this machine, each tied to evidence. Include numbers (cadence, number of ad variants, page templates).

## Unknowns & how to check
Bullet list: what we couldn't see, why, and the manual-check link.
