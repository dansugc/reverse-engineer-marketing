# Playbook: Founder-led distribution

- Identify founders/leaders (LinkedIn search + links on the site). Mark weak matches.
- Audience: LinkedIn followers, X followers. 10k+ and posting weekly = a real channel.
- Content: what the recent posts are about (building in public, hot takes, product launches, hiring, metrics). Articles/newsletters = long-form authority play.
- Connection to the company: do posts announce launches, share customer wins, or drive to a waitlist/demo? Do company ads promote founder posts (LinkedIn thought-leader ads)?
- Founder story in ads or on the about page = founder-as-brand.

Copyable output: posting cadence, the 2–3 recurring themes, and how posts route to the product.
