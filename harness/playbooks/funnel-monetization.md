# Playbook: Funnel, offer and monetization

- **Entry offer:** free plan, free trial (length), freemium credits, lead magnet, quiz, free tool, demo. The CTA they repeat on the homepage is the funnel's front door.
- **Pricing model:** per seat, usage/credits, tiers, lifetime deal, annual discount, money-back guarantee. Contact-sales tier = sales-assisted upmarket motion.
- **Motion:** self-serve (PLG) vs sales-led (demo page, Calendly/Chili Piper, visitor-identification tools like RB2B/Warmly/Koala/Clearbit) vs hybrid.
- **Lifecycle:** email/CRM tools (HubSpot, Customer.io, Klaviyo, Loops, Braze…). Most lifecycle runs server-side, so absence is unknown, not none.
- **Affiliate/referral:** program page, commission terms, tracking tool (Rewardful, PartnerStack, FirstPromoter, Tolt, Dub, impact.com).
- **Social proof used:** user counts, ratings, logos, "as seen on", review widgets.
- **Experimentation:** Optimizely/VWO/Statsig/GrowthBook = they A/B test the funnel.

Copyable output: the offer to copy, the pricing structure, and the one funnel step most worth replicating.
