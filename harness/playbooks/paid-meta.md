# Playbook: Paid Meta (Facebook + Instagram)

What to extract:
- **Volume & velocity:** total ads in library, active count, ads launched in the last 30 days. High launch rate = creative-volume strategy (Advantage+ / broad targeting, creative is the targeting).
- **Winners:** longest-running active ads. Quote their hook (first line of body), angle and CTA. 60+ days = proven.
- **Variants:** collation_count > 1 means many versions of the same ad (dynamic creative / copy tests).
- **Formats:** VIDEO vs IMAGE vs CAROUSEL vs DCO. Video-heavy with talking heads usually means UGC creators.
- **Angles:** classify each winner: problem/agitation, social proof, before/after, founder story, offer/discount, feature demo, comparison, "us vs them", listicle, testimonial, POV skit.
- **CTA + landing:** where ads send traffic (homepage vs dedicated landing page vs app store vs quiz vs lead magnet). Dedicated pages + UTMs = a mature funnel.
- **UTM campaign names:** they leak campaign structure (e.g. utm_campaign=startups → a startup program).
- **Placements:** FB vs IG vs Audience Network vs Messenger.

Copyable output: number of new creatives/week to match, the 2–3 angles to test first, the landing page pattern, the offer.
