# Playbook: SEO and AI search (GEO)

- **Size & shape:** total sitemap URLs and the biggest folders. A folder with hundreds/thousands of same-shaped slugs = programmatic SEO. Name the template (e.g. "/templates/{use-case}", "/integrations/{app}", "/{a}-vs-{b}", "/tools/{free-tool}").
- **Comparison & alternatives pages:** "/x-vs-y" and "/alternatives/x" = they intercept competitor demand (bottom of funnel).
- **Free tools / calculators / generators:** lead-gen magnets that rank.
- **Content cadence:** content URLs updated in 30/90 days. Big sitemap + low freshness = legacy asset.
- **Locales:** /de/, /fr/… = international SEO.
- **Money pages:** top Google results for site:domain ≈ what Google ranks them for first.
- **AI search:** llms.txt published? AI bots blocked or allowed in robots.txt? Allowing GPTBot/ClaudeBot/PerplexityBot + llms.txt = actively courting AI citations.

Copyable output: the 1–2 page templates to build first and the number of pages, plus AI-crawler settings.
