# Lesson library

Format: **cue** → lesson. (Evidence that taught us.) Add lessons when an eval or a human review shows a recurring miss.

- **Ad active 60+ days** → treat as a proven winner; nobody keeps paying for a loser. Lead the Paid section with these. (Long-running Meta ads correlate with scaled spend.)
- **Many new ads in the last 30 days + many variants (collation_count)** → active creative testing; the company is scaling Meta with a creative-volume strategy. Report launch rate.
- **Same hook in organic top posts and in ads** → "organic-first testing" loop: they post organically, then put spend behind winners. Call it out; it's the most copyable play.
- **Meta Pixel installed but zero ads in the library** → past or paused spend, or ads run from another page/brand. Say so; don't claim active spend.
- **Google ads attributed to a different advertiser name** → resellers, affiliates or an agency entity bid on the brand. Flag it.
- **Programmatic page family (hundreds of /x-vs-y, /templates/, /integrations/ pages)** → SEO is a core engine even if the blog is quiet. Describe the template.
- **Big sitemap, few fresh URLs** → legacy SEO asset still compounding; new content isn't the engine.
- **Founder with 10k+ followers posting weekly** → founder-led distribution; quote what they post about and connect it to launches.
- **Demo page + visitor-identification tag (RB2B, Warmly, Koala, Clearbit)** → outbound/ABM motion; the website feeds a sales team.
- **AppsFlyer/Adjust/Branch + App Store app with thousands of ratings** → app install campaigns (Meta/TikTok/Apple Search Ads) are likely even if the ad library shows little.
- **Affiliate page + Rewardful/PartnerStack/FirstPromoter** → affiliate channel; report commission terms if visible.
- **Homepage repositioned between snapshots** → note the pivot and the new ICP; ads and SEO usually follow within months.
- **Reddit threads asking "X vs Y" or complaining** → community demand exists; check if the company replies (founder accounts) or runs Reddit ads.
- **No evidence for a channel** → it's unknown, not absent. Never write "they don't do X" without a negative observation (e.g. ad library checked and empty).
