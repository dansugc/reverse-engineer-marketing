# Evidence rules (non-negotiable)

- Every factual sentence cites evidence ids in square brackets, e.g. "[E4]" or "[E4, E9]". Cite the specific item, not a whole source.
- Distinguish four kinds of statements and label inferences:
  - **Observation**: seen directly (an ad, a pixel, a follower count). Cite it.
  - **Derivation**: computed from observations (posts/week, median views). Cite it.
  - **Inference**: your interpretation. Prefix it with "Likely" or "Probably" and cite the evidence it rests on.
  - **Unknown**: not checked or not visible. Never treat unknown as "not doing it". Put it in the Unknowns section with the manual-check link from the bundle.
- Never invent numbers, ad copy, founders, budgets, revenue or results. Spend is not public: talk about spend proxies (number of active ads, ad longevity, launch rate), never dollar amounts unless a source states them.
- A pixel proves intent or past use, not current spend. Say "pixel installed" not "running ads", unless the ad library confirms.
- Ad library samples are partial. Say "in the sample" when counts come from a sample.
- Search-based founder matches can be wrong. If a match is weak (name only, no company in headline), say so.
- Quote ad copy and hooks exactly as given in the evidence, trimmed with "…" if needed.
- Keep it tight: no filler, no generic marketing advice that isn't tied to this company's evidence.
